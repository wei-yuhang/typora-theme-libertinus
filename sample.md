# Libertinus

*A quiet page for thoughtful writing.*

Libertinus brings the character of a typeset article to everyday Markdown: a generous reading measure, expressive serif letters, and a restrained palette of ink, paper, and warm grey.

## The shape of an idea

Good typography gives a document structure without getting in the way. **Clear headings**, *gentle emphasis*, and [useful links](https://github.com/wei-yuhang/typora-theme-libertinus) help a reader move naturally through the page.

> A page should invite the reader in, then let the words do the work.

### Details that matter

| Element | Treatment |
| :--- | :--- |
| Body text | Libertinus Serif |
| Quotations | Italic text and a grey rule |
| Tables | Simple, booktabs-style rules |

## From words to code

Small examples belong beside the ideas they explain. Inline `code` stays upright, even inside a quotation.

```python
def mean(values):
    return sum(values) / len(values)

print(mean([2, 4, 6]))
```

## A little mathematics

For a sequence of values, the arithmetic mean is

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i.
$$

The formula and the code express the same idea in different ways.

## Across languages

中文排版同样需要清晰的层次与舒适的留白。This theme includes a serif fallback chain for Chinese text; the available fonts depend on your system.

Latin and Chinese characters can coexist in the same paragraph. Font fallbacks are chosen for each character, letting both scripts keep their own typographic character.
